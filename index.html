About Dream Room

Dream Room is a calm interior design game. Clients send you a short brief, and you pick the bed, walls, floor, art and more to match their style, colours and budget, then drag every piece to just the right spot.

Each room is scored on style, colours, must-haves and completeness, with bonuses for spending wisely and little touches like plants and pets. Play 32 client rooms over five chapters, a new Daily Brief every day, and decorate your own room, sticker scenes and design studio with the coins you earn.

3 quick tips

  1. Check the must-haves first. Missing one caps you at 1 star, and 3 stars also need every must-have.
  2. Tap the client's face to ask for help. Little hearts then show the pieces and colours they love.
  3. Leave 15% of the budget unspent for the Thrifty bonus, but still fill every spot.
